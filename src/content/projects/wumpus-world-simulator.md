highlights:
  - Play mode with keyboard or an on-screen D-pad, and an AI Agent mode driven by inference rules K1–K11.
  - A Reasoning panel logs every inference in plain language, with a Safe / Maybe pit / Maybe Wumpus overlay on the board.
  - Seeded levels that are validated to be solvable, with shareable links, three difficulties, and two scoring modes.
  - Pixel-art graphics and chiptune sounds are generated in code, with no asset files.
  - Dark, light, and system themes, a colour-blind mode, reduced-motion support, and English and Bahasa Indonesia.
caseStudy:

  outcome: A playable, shareable simulator that makes the agent's logical reasoning visible step by step.

Wumpus World Simulator is an experimental university project: a browser version
of the classic Wumpus World with a human-playable mode and a logic-based agent
whose reasoning can be followed in real time.
